How to read this

Consensus moves aggregate dollar changes across every tracked manager between their two most recent 13F filings. A position in buys means the group added money on net; sells means they trimmed or exited on net.

Your copy shows what that position would be worth in your portfolio if you mirrored the managers equally: it uses the average portfolio weight the managers who hold it assign to that stock, times your portfolio size.

Signals per manager: NEW position opened, EXIT sold out completely, INCREASED / REDUCED share count changed by 5% or more.

Freshness: 13F filings are quarterly and must be filed within 45 days of quarter end. Check in daily — this page pulls the latest filings from SEC EDGAR, refreshing its cache every 12 hours, or hit Refresh to force a pull right now. The manager list is in engine.py.

Disclaimer: educational tool only, not financial advice. 13F data lags the market by up to 45 days and managers may have traded since filing.
