PSL Dictionary

Trader edition.

Mog
To dominate. A position at or past its target is mogging. A market that runs your stop has mogged you.
PSL scale
1 to 8. Here it maps unrealized R: under −1R is "It's over", 0R is LTN/MTN, +3R and up is PSL God.
Frauding
Looking like a setup, trading like a bag. Within 0.25R of the stop.
It's over / We're so back
Drawdown over 5% from peak / equity at an all-time high. Both are temporary.
Mewing
Keeping your mouth shut and waiting for price to hit your trigger. Also the 1% default risk.
Softmaxx / Hardmaxx
0.5% risk while cold or unsure; 2% only for A+ setups in a strong tape.
Canthal tilt
Reward-to-risk. Positive tilt is 2R or better at target. Negative tilt is below 1.5R; skip it.
Cortisol
Open heat: what you lose if every stop hits right now, as % of equity. Keep it under 6%.
Copemaxxing
Moving a stop down because you "believe in it". Breaks the Frame streak.
Leanmaxxing
Cutting losers fast so the average loss stays small. Fixes a weak midface (profit factor).
Halo effect
One big winner making you think every trade is genius. Size stays the same after a heater.
Looksmatch
Positions that move together. Five semis is one trade at five times the size.
